# Feedback Operator

## Who

A support or product team member who receives feedback from an MCP application
through an existing database, GitHub Issues, or Zendesk workflow.

## Context Constraints

- They need their existing assignment, status, notification, and reporting tools.
- They need exact thread correlation across multiple replies.
- A client receipt proves an offer to the AI, not human comprehension.

## Pain Points

- A separate feedback inbox fragments support work.
- Mutable one-message records lose conversation history.
- Replies are hard to action when nobody can tell whether they reached the AI.
