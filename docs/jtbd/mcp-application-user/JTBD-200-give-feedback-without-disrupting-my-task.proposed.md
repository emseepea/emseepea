# JTBD-200: Give Feedback Without Disrupting My Task

## Job Statement

When something is notably helpful, difficult, surprising, or wrong, I want
useful feedback recorded openly and any response brought back to me, so I can
continue my task without completing a survey or managing a ticket.

## Desired Outcomes

- Record one useful observation without asking a separate permission question.
- Tell me in the same response what was recorded.
- Stop recording if I object and avoid reporting the same observation twice.
- Bring a support reply into the conversation without requiring an acknowledgement action.
- Preserve enough detail to explain what helped, failed, surprised, or caused friction.

## Persona Constraints

The feedback flow must not capture the surrounding conversation or distract
from the user's task.

## What Users Do Today

Users abandon feedback, complete a separate form, or repeat the issue to a
support channel with no connection to the original conversation.
